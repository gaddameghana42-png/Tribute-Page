About Mother Teresa

Mother Teresa was a Catholic nun and humanitarian. She founded the Missionaries of Charity in 1950 in Kolkata, India. She worked to support people who were poor, sick and in need of care.

Early Life

Mother Teresa was born on 26 August 1910 in Skopje. Her birth name was Anjezë Gonxhe Bojaxhiu. She came to India in 1929 and later began her charitable work in Kolkata.

Her Great Work

  • Helped poor and homeless people.
  • Provided care to sick and abandoned people.
  • Founded the Missionaries of Charity in 1950.
  • Inspired people to serve their communities.

Awards and Achievements

  • Nobel Peace Prize – 1979
  • Bharat Ratna – 1980
  • Canonized as Saint Teresa of Calcutta – 2016

A Tribute to Her

Her life reminds us that kindness, compassion and service can make a difference in the world.
